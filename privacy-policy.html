Privacy Policy

Cubes & Crowns: Boss Puzzle · Last updated: 31 August 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Cubes & Crowns: Boss Puzzle ("the Game") handles, why, and what choices you have. The Game is published by TEAMPLUS108 ("we", "us"), trading as TP108 Studio.

The Game has no accounts, no sign-in, and no social features. We do not ask you for your name, email address, phone number, or location.

Information handled by the Game

Your game progress — levels, coins, purchased items, equipped theme, daily-login streak, and settings — is stored on your device only. We do not upload it or copy it to our servers. Uninstalling the Game deletes it.

Information handled by third-party services

The Game uses services from Google and Unity. These services collect some data automatically so that ads and purchases can work.

ServiceWhat it handlesPurpose
Google AdMob Advertising ID, coarse device and app information, IP address, ad interaction data Showing rewarded and interstitial ads, measuring them, and preventing fraud
Google Play Billing Purchase records tied to your Google account Processing in-app purchases and restoring non-consumable items
Unity (Gaming Services / In-App Purchasing) Device identifiers and technical diagnostic data Delivering the purchase flow and diagnosing crashes

We never receive your payment card details. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play.

Their policies apply alongside this one:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Unity Player Privacy Policy

Advertising choices

Ads may be personalised based on your Advertising ID. You can change this at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads, where you can delete or reset your Advertising ID and opt out of personalised ads.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland: the Game shows a consent screen the first time you play. You can reopen it from the Settings menu inside the Game and change your answer.

Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us and we will remove it.

Data retention and deletion

We hold no personal data ourselves, so there is nothing for us to delete on request. To remove local game data, uninstall the Game or clear its storage in your device settings. To ask Google or Unity about data they hold, use the links above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you, or to object to its processing. Because the data described above sits with Google and Unity rather than with us, direct such requests to them, or write to us and we will point you to the right place.

Changes to this policy

If the Game starts handling data differently, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Continuing to play after an update means you accept the revised policy.
