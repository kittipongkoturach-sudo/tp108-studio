Privacy Policy

Crown Mart: Pack & Ship · Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Crown Mart: Pack & Ship ("the Game") handles, why, and what choices you have. The Game is published by TEAMPLUS108 ("we", "us"), trading as TP108 Studio.

The Game has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask you for your name, email address, phone number, or location.

Information handled by the Game

Your game progress — levels, stars, coins, helpers, shop decorations, daily gifts, and settings — is stored on your device only. We do not upload it or copy it to our servers. Uninstalling the Game deletes it.

The Game does not send gameplay analytics to us, and it does not sell anything for real money.

Information handled by third-party services

The Game shows ads from Google. Google's advertising service collects some data automatically so that ads can be shown, measured, and protected against fraud.

ServiceWhat it handlesPurpose
Google AdMob Advertising ID, coarse device and app information, IP address (approximate location), ad interaction data, and diagnostic data Showing rewarded and interstitial ads, measuring them, and preventing fraud

Some ads are optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch for an in-game reward, such as extra coins or a free table slot.

Google's policies apply alongside this one:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Advertising choices

Ads may be personalised based on your Advertising ID. You can change this at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads, where you can delete or reset your Advertising ID and opt out of personalised ads.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland: the Game shows a consent screen the first time you play. You can change your answer at any time from Pause → Privacy settings inside the Game.

Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data retention and deletion

We do not keep personal data on our own servers. To remove local game data, uninstall the Game or clear its storage in your device settings. Data collected by Google for advertising is kept under Google's own retention rules; use the links above or contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you, or to object to its processing. Because the data described above sits with Google rather than with us, direct such requests to Google, or write to us and we will point you to the right place.

Changes to this policy

If the Game starts handling data differently — for example, if we add in-app purchases or analytics — we will update this page and change the date at the top. Continuing to play after an update means you accept the revised policy.
