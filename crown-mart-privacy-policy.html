Privacy Policy

Crown Mart: Pack & Ship · Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Crown Mart: Pack & Ship ("the Game") handles, why, and what choices you have. The Game is published by TEAMPLUS108 ("we", "us"), trading as TP108 Studio.

The Game has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask you for your name, email address, phone number, or location.

Information handled by the Game

Your game progress — levels, stars, coins, helpers, shop decorations, daily gifts, and settings — is stored on your device only. We do not upload it or copy it to our servers. Uninstalling the Game deletes it.

While you play, the Game sends gameplay events (for example, which level you started, whether you won, lost or left it, how long it took, which helpers and shop items you used, and which rewarded videos you chose to watch) to Google Analytics for Firebase. These events are tied to a random app-instance ID, not to your name or email, and we only look at them as aggregated reports to improve difficulty and features. If the Game crashes, a crash report is sent to Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix it.

Information handled by third-party services

The Game uses services from Google. These services collect some data automatically so that ads, the optional purchase, analytics and crash reports can work.

ServiceWhat it handlesPurpose
Google AdMob Advertising ID, coarse device and app information, IP address (approximate location), ad interaction data, and diagnostic data Showing rewarded and interstitial ads, measuring them, and preventing fraud
Google Analytics for Firebase App-instance ID, Advertising ID, device and app information, approximate location (country/region derived from IP address), and in-game events Understanding how the Game is played so we can improve it
Firebase Crashlytics Crash reports: device and app information, the state of the app when it crashed, and an installation ID Finding and fixing crashes
Google Play Billing (through Unity In-App Purchasing) Purchase records tied to your Google account Processing the optional "No Ads" purchase and restoring it on a new device

We never receive your payment card details. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play.

Some ads are optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch for an in-game reward, such as extra coins or a free table slot.

Google's policies apply alongside this one:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Unity Player Privacy Policy

Advertising choices

Ads may be personalised based on your Advertising ID. You can change this at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads, where you can delete or reset your Advertising ID and opt out of personalised ads.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland: the Game shows a consent screen the first time you play. You can change your answer at any time from Pause → Privacy settings inside the Game.

Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data retention and deletion

We do not keep personal data on our own servers. Gameplay events are stored by Google Analytics for Firebase for the retention period set in our Analytics account (no longer than 14 months) and then deleted; only aggregated reports remain. Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days. To remove local game data, uninstall the Game or clear its storage in your device settings. Data collected by Google for advertising is kept under Google's own retention rules; use the links above or contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you, or to object to its processing. Because the data described above sits with Google and Unity rather than with us, direct such requests to them, or write to us and we will point you to the right place.

Changes to this policy

If the Game starts handling data differently, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Continuing to play after an update means you accept the revised policy.
